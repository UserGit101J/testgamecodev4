This is a game that helps people learn about history, 
and create habits of always looking 
at the bigger picture when it comes down to history.
the player presses space to jump over the obsicales and the player must collect
all of the good history artifacts to win.
you play as a character named "history ball" whose goal is to collect all of the good artifacts and 
avoid the bad ones.

The items on the bottom of the screen represent "bad artifacts". Bad artifacts are things in history that give false information. 
The "good artifacts" on top that you have to jump to and get points from represent good information. 


to launch the game open up  the "test game" folder and then open "game.py" via double click or right click and then press open.

All images are made by me using pixelart, Besides the background which was made using python pygame colors.
